`GET /v1/admin/audit` serves the decision log newest-first, keyset-paged, filtered by action, actor,
token and window. Rows carry who, what, when, the decision and its reason — metadata only, so no
secret or request body can ever leak out of it. Two deliberate rules: the read itself is never
audited (a read that wrote rows would grow the log on every page turn), and every denial is
recorded — a refused call is a decision, not silence.
